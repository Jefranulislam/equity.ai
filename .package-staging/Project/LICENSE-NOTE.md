## Hosted services

The product depends on two external services configured per-installation (not bundled here):

1. **Supabase** — PostgreSQL hosting + auth + storage (free tier available; your data, your account).
2. **AI provider** (optional, for the Freya chat) — OpenRouter (users supply their own key in Settings → AI); or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint configured via env vars.
